## The problem

A solo stylist or small salon cannot answer the phone mid-appointment. Missed calls become lost bookings, and evenings go to texting clients and fixing the calendar. Professionals also have to sanitize their hands after touching a phone before touching the next client, so a ringing phone is a lost booking or a safety problem. Booking apps handle online booking. None of them answers the phone.

## What it does

- **Booking and calendar:** a booking page for every salon, an owner and staff calendar, client history, checkout with tips and gift cards, and card payments inside the app.
- **SANAA, the AI receptionist:** answers the phone, checks real availability, books, reschedules and cancels, answers questions from the salon's own information, and hands off to a person when asked.
- **Apps for owners and clients:** iPhone and Android apps for owners and clients, plus a watch app. The aim is a fully hands-free front desk, with voice and wearable devices.

## Business model

- **Salon membership:** **$14.99 per month**, one flat price for the whole salon, unlimited staff.
- **Card payments:** a 1% platform fee (20 cents minimum) on payments taken through the app.
- **SANAA, the AI receptionist (add-on), priced by calling minutes.** Salons start with a **$5 trial** of 30 calling minutes or 7 days, whichever comes first, then pick a plan:

The **founding price** is the launch discount for our first salons. It cuts the regular price by 38% to 41% on every plan. The **regular price** is the standard rate. The bigger the plan, the less each calling minute costs.

- **SANAA for any appointment business** ([getsanaa.com/pricing](https://getsanaa.com/pricing)): plans from **$39 per month** with 150 calling minutes, after a **free 7-day test** (no card required).
- **Founding Member Credit:** a new salon earns a free month by connecting card payments and taking five card-paid bookings in its first 30 days. This pushes new salons to real use.
